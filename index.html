Before Recording

Important:

• Use HTTPS for browser capture.
• Desktop browsers generally offer the broadest screen-capture support.
• Many Android browsers cannot capture the entire device screen.
• System audio is not available for every source or browser.
• 4K composition and webcam overlays require significant processing power.
• Long recordings need substantial free storage space.
